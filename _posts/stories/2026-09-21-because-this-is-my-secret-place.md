These days are like rain drops on my purely exhausted soul. 

With an academic mind, a highly achiever soul, I thought "Ok, I'm good to go."

Time flies, wounds I thought were healed, my agony ended desperately. 


With such a good research background but also academically traumatized, I decided that I'm not going into my graduate journey as soon as I finish with my undergraduate study. I want to seek out work environments where I can finally exit from this, but still being able to do Math and research. So, perfect role fits my interest the most is being a Quant Researcher. 


Yes, I did it for 1 year, studied interview for WorldQuant for about 6 months, even during times that my asthma relapsed and I have to come to the hospital and get treated for 2 weeks, I still brought my laptop to do my work, cause I was so stressed that I might fail getting into this QR Internship with WQ. And? Well, I failed. Failed? No actually, cause I didn't even have the chance to fail it (tragically laughing with salt stream falling out of my eyes into my mouth) 


Cause guess what, after getting cured for my illness two times and finally had the strength to submit the CV so that an old friend can refer me, after TWO WEEKS of submitting, he got the news that they actually closed the Intern headcount position right before my submission only 1 day, cause they've elected someone from the IQC. And if I did not reach out to ask about the application status, they would have never responded to me. Only after my friend reached out, the head of HR said they have closed it and told him to verbally inform me. You can clearly picture the smile and the hope dropped on my face. 


I guess I was normally strong cause this is not the first time I fail or get the chance slipped out my hands. But guess why I cried? Cause this freaking year is so brutal to me nobody do hear about it enough. I nearly got sexually assaulted by a close friend and ran to save my freaking life, got PTSD and nearly killed myself. I got my asthma relapsed with 5 rounds a day. My brother is an addict and he shouted at my face every single day in May, June, July and August. I was practically okay when I was away from home but now I've graduated, I have to come home to search for a job. During this time, my neighbors gossip every single day about my employment status, and then my parents came back home to hint to me I'm a waste of investment. Just the thought of going to get employed saved me, and then my last hope got snatched out my hands by the winds of fate. Smiley face of me hides every pain of me. Guess I'm to accustomed to showing a bright and smiley face that people can either identify that I'm faking it, or see that I'm eternally grateful for this lively life of mine. 


Days like these feel like torture. Even when I'm wounded over all of my body and my soul, I still have no other choices but still fight for my life. Waking up and work like crazy to search for a Quant job, or at least a research position. Guess that if I have a senior who can help me, it's gonna be easier, but who can when I'm too tired to reach out? Should've applied to NTU when I graduated high school like my fellow classmates, I got the chances and I did not take it. Why did I think that I want to stay close to my family back at that time? Why did I eliminate my only way of running away from all of these traps that my family has set to me, to stay closer to them? 


I've been stuck in this high cortisol, survivor mode for 7 straight years. I moved away from home to Hanoi to just enter a gifted school, just to get humiliated and bullied, noting that not everybody there was awful, some people were still good to me and I'm still lingering. I jumped on the bus to cry cause I felt like I could not take it anymore, never knowing the destination, just straight out crying, I still remember the gloomy dark and depressing weather of that day that I felt like the sky is crying with me too. 


I think I'm burning alive but nobody sees the fire. Can anyone just pull me out of this? I think I'm drowning but everybody thinks I'm dancing.
